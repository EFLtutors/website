Our Approach

Understanding first.
Confidence follows.

Grades matter. So does how a young person feels about learning. Our approach helps students build understanding, develop stronger habits and recognise their own progress.

The EFL philosophy
“Practice builds understanding.
Understanding builds confidence.”

Three principles

At the heart of every lesson.

Confidence isn’t something we expect a student to bring with them. It grows as they understand more, practise with purpose and experience progress.

01

Practice

Students think, apply knowledge and learn from mistakes. Guided examples and focused questions make practice purposeful.

02

Understanding

We explain why an idea works, so students can build connections and approach unfamiliar questions more independently.

03

Confidence

Success, encouragement and seeing their own progress help students feel more capable of taking the next step.

In practice

What that looks like
during a lesson.

Clear teaching and a supportive environment make room for different starting points, questions and the confidence to try again.

Clear explanations

Break a complex topic into manageable steps. Connect a method with the idea behind it.

Purposeful practice

Move from guided examples to independent questions, using practice to build understanding.

Questions that build thinking

Encourage students to explain their reasoning and notice what they understand.

Feedback that moves learning forward

Treat mistakes as information. Identify the next step and recognise progress along the way.

65%

An individual starting point

Different learners.
The same respect.

65% of our students are neurodivergent or have additional support needs. We encourage parents to share what helps their child feel comfortable and learn well, so we can discuss appropriate support and fit.

We value effort, progress and perseverance. We want learners to feel comfortable asking questions and making mistakes.

Our tutors & team

Subject specialists.
People who connect.

A strong learning relationship begins with the right person. Every tutor is selected for their ability to connect with students, their specialist knowledge and values that align with EFL Tutors.

Our team brings together GTC Scotland-registered teachers, subject professionals and experienced graduates. Some tutors are registered teachers; every tutor holds a PVG.

01

Connection with students

We look for people who can build a positive connection and help students feel comfortable asking questions.

02

Specialist knowledge

Subject expertise matters. Tutors bring their knowledge and experience to the learning they support.

03

Shared values

We select tutors whose values align with our focus on understanding, purposeful practice and growing confidence.

Safeguarding

Care and responsibility.
At the heart of our work.

We take safeguarding seriously. Supporting young people means taking care over the people we work with and how our tuition is organised.

Every tutor holds a PVG as a member of the Protecting Vulnerable Groups scheme. Our team includes GTC Scotland-registered teachers, professionals and experienced graduates.
