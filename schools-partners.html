A practical first conversation

Define the need.
Agree the plan.

01

Understand your learners.

Discuss year groups, subject needs, learning barriers, goals and the context your team is working within.

02

Explore a suitable offer.

Consider group size, staffing, online delivery, timetable and budget. Any proposal would be tailored and agreed with your team.

03

Agree responsibilities.

Before starting, agree safeguarding requirements, tutor checks, referrals, consent, data handling, school contacts and reporting.

04

Review what matters.

Set out how attendance, learning progress and engagement would be reviewed, with a shared understanding of what success means.

This page invites partnership discussions. It does not claim existing school contracts, approved-provider status or an already commissioned service.

Tell us what your
students need.

A first enquiry can be simple. Share your school or organisation, year groups, subject priorities and preferred time frame. Please avoid including identifiable pupil information.

Useful things to discuss

  • Learning goals and intended student group
  • Subject and qualification coverage
  • Timetable and delivery arrangements
  • Accessibility and support needs
  • Safeguarding and procurement requirements
  • Budget, reporting and review points

A little clarity before you choose

Schools & Partners FAQs.

Do you already work with schools?

This draft reflects the ambition to develop school partnerships. It does not present existing contracts or case studies. We’d welcome a conversation about future opportunities.

Can you provide a tailored proposal?

We can discuss your requirements and explore a suitable scope. Any services, staffing, pricing and delivery arrangements would need to be confirmed and agreed before work begins.

What safeguarding information can we request?

Discuss your organisation’s requirements at the start. Tutor checks, safeguarding procedures, reporting routes, data handling and responsibilities must be reviewed and agreed before any delivery.

How would progress be reported?

The reporting approach would be agreed with your team, based on the learning goals and appropriate information-sharing arrangements. No standard school reporting package is claimed in this draft.
