A little clarity before you choose

Schools & Partners FAQs.

Do you already work with schools?

We’re developing our school partnerships and welcome conversations about future opportunities.

Can you provide a tailored proposal?

We can discuss your requirements and explore a suitable scope. Any services, staffing, pricing and delivery arrangements would need to be confirmed and agreed before work begins.

What safeguarding information can we request?

Every tutor holds a PVG, and our team includes GTC Scotland-registered teachers, professionals and experienced graduates. We use TutorCruncher to support the organisation and oversight of tuition. Discuss your school’s requirements at the start so safeguarding procedures, reporting routes, data handling and responsibilities can be reviewed and agreed before delivery.

How would progress be reported?

The reporting approach would be agreed with your team, based on the learning goals and appropriate information-sharing arrangements. We would agree the format and frequency before delivery.
