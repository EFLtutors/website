Before you start

Free assessment FAQs.

Who are these assessments for?

Students and parents who want a quick starting point for a conversation about learning. The starter questions use introductory National 5 topics or general subject skills. They do not cover every unit or every course choice.

Will this tell us an exam grade?

No. Six questions cannot predict an exam grade or replace a teacher’s assessment. The feedback shows which of these questions were answered correctly and explains the ideas behind them.

Do we need to give an email address?

No. These self-checks are free and open. You can see all feedback without an account, email address or booking.

What happens to the answers?

The assessment runs in your browser tab. Answers are not submitted to EFL Tutors or saved in an account. Leaving or reloading the page clears the attempt.

Can we use this if our child is in a different year group?

You can explore the questions, but remember the intended topic level. We support S1–S6, with S3–S6 as our specialism. Ask a tutor or teacher which topics are appropriate for your child’s stage.
