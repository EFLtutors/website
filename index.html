A little clarity before you choose

Questions parents ask.

How do I know that EFL Tutors is a reputable company and ensures quality private tuition?

EFL Tutors is one of the most reputable tutoring agencies in Scotland, having served hundreds of students over the past 6 years. Our reviews on Google Business, and on TutorCruncher highlight our commitment to providing outstanding service. If you’re ever dissatisfied with a session, we offer a full refund for the lesson.

Read our reviews →
What’s a Booster Class?

Weekly online tuition for National 5, usually with 4–8 students in a 75-minute class. See all Booster Class FAQs →

How much do Booster Classes cost?

All Booster Classes cost £75 per student, per month. Ask about current availability, joining arrangements and payment terms before booking.

Can you support families anywhere in Scotland?

Yes. Our tuition is online, so you can access support across Scotland without travelling. Ask about availability for your subject and year group.

What happens in a free consultation?

We’ll talk about your child’s goals, strengths and challenges, answer your questions and explain the options available. There’s no obligation to book tuition.
