A little clarity before you choose

Booster Class FAQs.

How much do Booster Classes cost?

Every Booster Class is £75 per student, per month. Ask about joining dates, the payment schedule and any minimum booking or cancellation terms before enrolment. Those terms will be confirmed before you book.

How do Quick Checks fit into Booster Classes?

Try a free Quick Check before joining, then save the feedback to discuss with your tutor. It helps identify a focus for practice. Review learning with fresh questions and tutor feedback; the same six questions alone cannot measure improvement. Try a Quick Check →

How big are the groups?

Our classes typically include 4–8 students. This gives tutors space to get to know students, encourage participation and support their learning.

How long is a class?

Classes are weekly, 75-minute live online sessions during the academic year. The timetable shows the start time in UK local time.

Is my child suited to a small group?

Students work towards the same qualification; they do not need to be at exactly the same ability. We discuss your child’s needs first. If individual tuition looks like a better fit, we’ll explain that option.

What if my child is anxious or has additional support needs?

Tell us what helps them feel comfortable and learn well. Our aim is a welcoming, encouraging environment where questions and mistakes are part of learning. We’ll discuss their needs and the class fit during a Parent Consultation.

What happens if they miss a session?

Lesson materials are provided for catching up. Ask how this works for your child’s class before joining.

When can my child join?

Most classes follow the academic year, and spaces may become available during the year. Enquire about the current start date and whether joining now would be a good fit.

Are these one-off revision classes?

The featured Booster Classes are structured weekly support through the school year. Revision and exam practice are part of the learning journey; these are not advertised as single drop-in sessions.

What if the class is on a waiting list?

Register your interest using the timetable. This does not reserve a place or take a payment. We’ll discuss options when availability is confirmed.

Do you offer Booster Classes for every year group?

The featured timetable is for National 5. We support students from S1 to S6 across our tuition options, with S3–S6 as our specialism. Ask about other subjects and stages.

1:1 Tutoring FAQs.

How are tutors matched to my child?

Following your Parent Consultation, we consider subject, level, learning needs and personality. Feeling comfortable with a tutor matters alongside subject expertise.

How often should we have sessions?

Many families choose weekly sessions for consistency. We’ll discuss your child’s goals, workload and availability before recommending a schedule.

Can you support students with additional support needs?

Many tutors have experience supporting students with additional support needs, including ADHD, autism, dyslexia and anxiety. Share what your child needs so we can discuss appropriate support and tutor fit.

How does online tuition work?

Ask about your tutor’s online setup, including whiteboards, digital resources and any equipment needed before the first session.

What if the tutor isn’t the right fit?

Let us know. We can discuss whether an alternative tutor is available.

How does 1:1 compare with Booster Classes?

Booster Classes are £75 per month and offer a weekly small-group learning journey. Premium 1:1 tuition provides individual attention and a tailored focus, with hourly rates by qualification level.

Before you start

Quick Check FAQs.

Who are Quick Checks for?

Students and parents who want a quick starting point for a conversation about learning. The questions progress through two easy (C/D-style), two medium (B-style) and two hard (A-style) questions. These difficulty bands are guides for practice, not official grade allocations. They do not cover every unit or every course choice.

Can we use Quick Checks to measure improvement?

They give a starting snapshot and a topic to discuss. Save the feedback and review understanding with your tutor using fresh questions and lesson work. Repeating the same six questions is practice, not independent evidence of improvement.

Will this tell us an exam grade?

No. Six questions cannot predict an exam grade or replace a teacher’s assessment. The feedback shows which of these questions were answered correctly and explains the ideas behind them.

Do we need to sign up?

No. Quick Checks are free, with feedback available straight away.

What happens to the answers?

Answers stay in this browser tab and clear when you leave or reload. Use Save My Feedback to keep your own copy; nothing is automatically sent to EFL.

Can we use this if our child is in a different year group?

You can explore the questions, but remember the intended topic level. We support S1–S6, with S3–S6 as our specialism. Ask a tutor or teacher which topics are appropriate for your child’s stage.
