A little clarity before you choose

Questions parents ask.

What’s a Booster Class?

Booster Classes are our weekly small-group tuition. Our featured National 5 classes run online, with weekly 75-minute sessions and typically 4–8 students. They provide structured learning through the school year, rather than a single revision session.

Is EFL Tutors the same business as Edinburgh, Fife & Lothians Tutors?

Yes. EFL Tutors is the shorter name for the business Emily founded in 2020. Our roots are in Edinburgh, Fife and the Lothians; our online tuition supports families across Scotland.

Do you only support S3–S6?

No. We help learners from S1 through to S6. S3–S6 is our specialism. Ask about the subjects and support available for your child’s stage; the Booster Classes currently featured are at National 5 level.

How much do Booster Classes cost?

All Booster Classes cost £75 per student, per month. Ask about current availability, joining arrangements and payment terms before booking.

Will my child feel comfortable in a group?

Our current classes typically have 4–8 students working towards the same qualification. They don’t need to be at exactly the same ability. Tell us about their confidence and support needs so we can discuss the class fit or whether 1:1 tuition would be more suitable.

Can you support families anywhere in Scotland?

Yes. Our tuition is online, so you can access support across Scotland without travelling. Ask about availability for your subject and year group.

What happens in a free consultation?

We’ll talk about your child’s goals, strengths and challenges, answer your questions and explain the options available. There’s no obligation to book tuition.

A little clarity before you choose

Booster Class FAQs.

How much do Booster Classes cost?

Every Booster Class is £75 per student, per month. Ask about joining dates, the payment schedule and any minimum booking or cancellation terms before enrolment. Those terms will be confirmed before you book.

How big are the groups?

Our classes typically include 4–8 students. This gives tutors space to get to know students, encourage participation and support their learning.

How long is a class?

Classes are weekly, 75-minute live online sessions during the academic year. The timetable shows the start time in UK local time.

Is my child suited to a small group?

Students work towards the same qualification; they do not need to be at exactly the same ability. We discuss your child’s needs first. If individual tuition looks like a better fit, we’ll explain that option.

What if my child is anxious or has additional support needs?

Tell us what helps them feel comfortable and learn well. Our aim is a welcoming, encouraging environment where questions and mistakes are part of learning. We’ll discuss their needs and the class fit during a Parent Consultation.

What happens if they miss a session?

As described on our original website, lesson materials are provided so students can catch up on what was covered. Ask how this works for their class before joining.

When can my child join?

Most classes follow the academic year, and spaces may become available during the year. Enquire about the current start date and whether joining now would be a good fit.

Are these one-off revision classes?

The featured Booster Classes are structured weekly support through the school year. Revision and exam practice are part of the learning journey; these are not advertised as single drop-in sessions.

What if the class is on a waiting list?

Register your interest using the timetable. This does not reserve a place or take a payment. We’ll discuss options when availability is confirmed.

Do you offer Booster Classes for every year group?

The featured timetable is for National 5. We support students from S1 to S6 across our tuition options, with S3–S6 as our specialism. Ask about other subjects and stages.

1:1 Tutoring FAQs.

How are tutors matched to my child?

Following your Parent Consultation, we consider subject, level, learning needs and personality. Feeling comfortable with a tutor matters alongside subject expertise.

How often should we have sessions?

Many families choose weekly sessions for consistency. We’ll discuss your child’s goals, workload and availability before recommending a schedule.

Can you support students with additional support needs?

Many tutors have experience supporting students with additional support needs, including ADHD, autism, dyslexia and anxiety, as described on our existing site. Share what your child needs so we can discuss appropriate support and tutor fit.

How does online tuition work?

Our original site describes shared whiteboards, digital resources and collaborative tools. Ask about the platform, equipment and setup for your child’s tutor before the first session.

What if the tutor isn’t the right fit?

Let us know. Our original site offers help arranging an alternative tutor, and we’ll discuss the options available.

How does 1:1 compare with Booster Classes?

Booster Classes are £75 per month and offer a weekly small-group learning journey. Premium 1:1 tuition provides individual attention and a tailored focus, with hourly rates by qualification level.

The EFL philosophy

Three principles

At the heart of every lesson.

Confidence isn’t something we expect a student to bring with them. It grows as they understand more, practise with purpose and experience progress.

01

Practice

Students think, apply knowledge and learn from mistakes. Guided examples and focused questions make practice purposeful.

02

Understanding

We explain why an idea works, so students can build connections and approach unfamiliar questions more independently.

03

Confidence

Success, encouragement and seeing their own progress help students feel more capable of taking the next step.

In practice

What that looks like
during a lesson.

Clear teaching and a supportive environment make room for different starting points, questions and the confidence to try again.

Clear explanations

Break a complex topic into manageable steps. Connect a method with the idea behind it.

Purposeful practice

Move from guided examples to independent questions, using practice to build understanding.

Questions that build thinking

Encourage students to explain their reasoning and notice what they understand.

Feedback that moves learning forward

Treat mistakes as information. Identify the next step and recognise progress along the way.

Before you start

Free assessment FAQs.

Who are these assessments for?

Students and parents who want a quick starting point for a conversation about learning. The questions progress through two easy (C/D-style), two medium (B-style) and two hard (A-style) questions. These difficulty bands are guides for practice, not official grade allocations. They do not cover every unit or every course choice.

Will this tell us an exam grade?

No. Six questions cannot predict an exam grade or replace a teacher’s assessment. The feedback shows which of these questions were answered correctly and explains the ideas behind them.

Do we need to give an email address?

No. These self-checks are free and open. You can see all feedback without an account, email address or booking.

What happens to the answers?

The assessment runs in your browser tab. Answers are not submitted to EFL Tutors or saved in an account. Leaving or reloading the page clears the attempt.

Can we use this if our child is in a different year group?

You can explore the questions, but remember the intended topic level. We support S1–S6, with S3–S6 as our specialism. Ask a tutor or teacher which topics are appropriate for your child’s stage.
